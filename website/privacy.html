Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Overview & No Personal Data

PowerPulse ("we", "our", or "app") respects your privacy. We do not require any user account registration, login, or personal profile creation. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, bill reference numbers, consumer IDs, or passwords on our servers.

2. Anonymous Analytics & Counters

Our backend database uses anonymous, aggregate daily counters (e.g., total app opens or total bill check sessions) without recording any device identifiers, IP addresses, or personal tracking cookies.

3. Local Device Storage

All bill reminders, calculator preferences, and user inputs are stored locally on your device storage and are never sent to any external server.

4. Third-Party Bill Checking & WebView

When checking electricity bills, the app opens official DISCO and government web portals directly inside a secure WebView. These external websites are governed by their own respective privacy policies and terms of service.

5. Advertising (AdMob)

The app may display ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device advertising identifiers and cookies in accordance with Google's privacy policies to serve relevant advertisements.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at support@bijlibillchecker.pk.
