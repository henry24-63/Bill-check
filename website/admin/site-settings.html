🔒 App Privacy Policy Control

Manage the Privacy Policy shown inside the mobile app's Settings > Privacy Policy page. Changes take effect live in the mobile app immediately without needing an app update.

Default: https://bijlibillchecker.pk/privacy.html. Opens when user taps "Official Web Policy" or "Browser".
If filled, this text is rendered prominently in the mobile app. If left blank, the app displays its standard clean privacy protection guide.
